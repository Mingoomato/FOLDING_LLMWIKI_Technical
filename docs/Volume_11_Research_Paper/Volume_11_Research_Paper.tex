\chapter{Research Paper: LLMWiki - A Semantic Operating Layer for Evidence-Aware Knowledge Management}
\label{cha:paper}

%\title{LLMWiki: A Semantic Operating Layer for Evidence-Aware Knowledge Management}
%\author{Anonymous}
%\date{\\today}

\begin{abstract}
We present LLMWiki, a semantic operating layer that transforms heterogeneous digital artifacts into an evidence-aware knowledge space. Unlike Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems that treat vector search as primary and evidence as optional, LLMWiki enforces \emph{evidence closure}: every answer must trace to cross-encoder-verified units. The architecture rests on five pillars: (1) a property knowledge graph as canonical representation with vectors as projections; (2) a filesystem-backed write-ahead log enabling deterministic replay via directory synchronization; (3) content-addressed semantic units (SHA-256) providing identity across indices; (4) a mandatory evidence gate that filters retrieval results through calibrated cross-encoder scoring; and (5) a DAG-based query planner with cost-based optimization. A deterministic evidence-context boundary then selects verified units under a token budget and transmits them through a lossless, token-lean WC/1 envelope whose citations resolve to exact source occurrences. We specify an evaluation protocol across six benchmarks spanning code, APIs, schemas, documents, and configurations, and report preliminary internal targets suggesting higher Evidence F1 and lower hallucination rate versus strong baselines (GraphRAG, HippoRAG, LightRAG) while operating fully locally with zero external API dependencies. The transport reduction is tokenizer-measured, but model understanding and end-to-end quality remain unverified; the broader performance targets are not yet supported by a public, reproducible evaluation (\S\ref{sec:paper:results}) and should be treated as design goals, not verified results.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:paper:intro}

The proliferation of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems has established a dominant paradigm: embed chunks, retrieve top-$k$, synthesize with LLM. However, this paradigm suffers from systematic failure modes: hallucinated citations, retrieval of irrelevant but semantically similar chunks, inability to verify multi-hop claims, and dependence on external LLM APIs for the critical path.

We argue that these are not bugs but architectural consequences of treating vector similarity as a proxy for evidential relevance. Vector search optimizes for semantic proximity in embedding space, which correlates with but does not guarantee relevance to a specific query. The gap between "semantically similar" and "evidentially supportive" is precisely where hallucinations enter.

LLMWiki addresses this by promoting evidence verification to a first-class architectural invariant. The system is a \emph{semantic operating layer}---an intermediary between raw artifacts and LLM synthesis that guarantees every synthesized token traces to a verified unit.

Promoting verification to an invariant is necessary but not sufficient, and the substance of this paper is what the invariant requires once taken seriously. Three questions follow immediately and have no answer in the retrieval literature. First, at what score does a unit count as verified, given that relevance annotations are the scarcest resource in any real deployment? Second, why should a single thresholded score be the right discriminator at all, when the score distributions of relevant and irrelevant units overlap? Third, what guarantees that units which each pass verification are mutually consistent, rather than individually plausible and jointly contradictory?

Each question has a mature answer in a field that is not information retrieval. Assigning significance without labels is the daily business of gravitational-wave astronomy, which constructs empirical null distributions by displacing detector streams beyond physical coincidence \citep{was2010timeslides}. Exceeding the discrimination limit of a single equilibrium measurement is the problem kinetic proofreading was invented to solve \citep{hopfield1974kinetic,ninio1975kinetic}, and remains an active subject in immunology \citep{kirby2025parallel,multimolecular2026}. Deciding whether locally consistent data glues into a globally consistent whole is the definition of sheaf cohomology, now applied to distributed tasks and network consistency \citep{sheafdistributed2025,sheafnetworks2025}. Our contribution is to recognise these as the same problems and to transfer the mechanisms, not the metaphors.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Label-free significance for retrieval.} We construct empirical null distributions over query--unit pairs whose relevance is excluded by corpus, temporal, or graph displacement, and report every admitted unit's false alarm rate rather than a raw score (\S\ref{sec:paper:far}). To our knowledge no retrieval system calibrates without relevance labels.
  \item \textbf{Proofreading cascade.} We replace single-threshold gating with $n$ irreversible discard stages over distinct evidence channels, obtaining false-acceptance rates that decay multiplicatively in depth at survival-weighted cost, and we state the accompanying compute--fidelity frontier rather than eliding it.
  \item \textbf{Cohomological evidence closure.} We define evidence closure as the existence of a global section of a cellular sheaf over the verified set, making mutual consistency a computable and localisable property; nonzero $H^1$ triggers abstention with a conflict witness.
  \item \textbf{Budget as allocation.} We schedule plan steps by expected evidential gain per unit cost, permitting early termination under budget --- behaviour unattainable by a circuit breaker.
  \item \textbf{Evidence-preserving context transport.} We separate admissibility, budgeted selection, and serialization: verified evidence remains verbatim, machine identifiers stay host-side, and every model-visible citation handle resolves to an exact source occurrence. We report token savings separately from the still-open model-quality A/B.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:paper:related}

\subsection{Retrieval-Augmented Generation}
The original RAG framework (Lewis et al., 2020) combines a frozen retriever (DPR) with a generator. Subsequent work improved retrieval (ANCE, Contriever), generation (FiD, ATLAS), or both. A common thread: retrieval is \emph{unverified}; the generator receives top-$k$ passages and must implicitly filter noise.

\subsection{Graph-Enhanced RAG}
GraphRAG (Edge et al., 2024) constructs a knowledge graph via LLM-based entity extraction and community detection. HippoRAG (Guti{\'e}rrez et al., 2024) uses personalized PageRank over a graph for retrieval. LightRAG (Guo et al., 2024) maintains a two-level graph (entity + chunk). These systems improve structural awareness but retain two limitations: (1) graphs are constructed by LLMs, introducing upstream hallucinations; (2) no mandatory evidence verification before synthesis.

\subsection{Evidence and Attribution}
Recent work on attribution (Gao et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024) evaluates citation quality but treats it as a metric, not a constraint. RARR (Gao et al., 2023) post-edits citations; our gate \emph{prevents} unverified units from reaching the generator.

\subsection{Prompt Compression and Context Selection}
LLMLingua and LongLLMLingua remove or reorder prompt content with learned
models, reporting large compression and latency gains
\citep{jiang2023llmlingua,jiang2024longllmlingua}. They change evidence text
and add a model to the critical path, which conflicts with byte-level citation
auditability and deterministic local replay. TOON instead targets structured
serialization overhead and shows that syntax savings must amortise a fixed
instruction ``prompt tax'' \citep{matveev2026toon}. WC/1 addresses the same
serialization class but only for read-only evidence envelopes and does not
claim format novelty.

Under a fixed reader budget, top-$k$ becomes a subset-selection policy rather
than merely a retriever setting. Budgeted submodular selection is established
in summarization \citep{lin2010budgeted}; recent RAG work confirms both the
value and the limited scope of redundancy-aware or submodular packing
\citep{peng2025adagres,bala2026survives}. The latter finding is load-bearing:
packing helps only under specific retrieval, budget, evidence-structure, and
reader-capacity conditions. LLMWiki therefore treats weighted concept coverage
as a proxy and requires end-to-end quality-at-budget measurement.

\subsection{Local and Deterministic Systems}
PrivateGPT, GPT4All, and similar projects enable local LLM inference but retain the RAG pipeline architecture. No prior system enforces deterministic replay via filesystem synchronization.

\subsection{Methods Imported from Adjacent Sciences}
\label{sec:paper:imported}
The mechanisms in \S\ref{sec:paper:far}--\ref{sec:paper:sheaf} originate outside machine learning, and we summarise their provenance so that the transfer can be audited rather than taken on faith.

\emph{Empirical nulls without labels.} Searches for compact binary coalescence assign significance to candidate events by the method of time slides: triggers from separate detectors are offset by more than the inter-detector light travel time, so that resulting coincidences cannot be astrophysical, and the accumulated sample defines the noise background as a function of the ranking statistic \citep{was2010timeslides,pycbclive2018}. The method's known limitation --- estimation error saturates with the number of shifts --- transfers along with the method, and we treat it as such.

\emph{Discrimination beyond the equilibrium bound.} Kinetic proofreading achieves recognition fidelity exceeding what binding-affinity differences permit, by interposing irreversible, energy-consuming intermediate steps \citep{hopfield1974kinetic,ninio1975kinetic}. Contemporary work establishes that parallel reaction threads improve fidelity without lengthening the sequential chain \citep{kirby2025parallel} and that multimolecular schemes relax the activity--fidelity trade-off \citep{multimolecular2026}; both results shape the cascade design of \S\ref{sec:paper:cascade}.

\emph{Gluing local data.} Cellular sheaves encode local data with consistency constraints over a graph; global sections are the consistent assignments and the first cohomology measures the obstruction to their existence. The construction has recently been applied to characterise solvable tasks in distributed systems \citep{sheafdistributed2025}, to consistency on network sheaves \citep{sheafnetworks2025}, and to irreducible error in predictive coding \citep{sheafpc2025}.

\emph{Scheduling under hard resource deficits.} Transient alert brokers rank candidates for follow-up when observing capacity is orders of magnitude below alert volume, using score-plus-observability prioritisation \citep{aas2rto2025}, active learning to maximise information gain \citep{fink2025active}, and reinforcement learning for online constrained scheduling \citep{rlfollowup2025}.

\emph{Lineage reconstruction from degraded records.} CRISPR recorders accumulate irreversible edits enabling reconstruction of division history from terminal states \citep{lineagetracing2026}, with Bayesian phylodynamic inference supplying calibrated uncertainty under missing and dependent observations \citep{phylodynamic2025}. The homoplasy problem --- independent identical edits implying false common ancestry --- has a direct analogue under content addressing.

\section{System Architecture}
\label{sec:paper:arch}

\subsection{Three-Layer Decomposition}
LLMWiki decomposes into three layers with strictly unidirectional data flow:

\textbf{Layer 1: Ingestion.} Parses artifacts $\to$ semantic units (tree-sitter for code, specialized parsers for OpenAPI, SQL, Markdown, PDF) $\to$ structure-aware chunking $\to$ local embedding (BGE-M3, E5, Nomic) $\to$ write-ahead log.

\textbf{Layer 2: Core.} Knowledge graph (property graph with entity resolution), hybrid index (HNSW + BM25 + graph adjacency), evidence gate (cross-encoder + calibration).

\textbf{Layer 3: Query.} DAG planner (decomposes query, assigns strategies, optimizes order), agent runtime (tool execution, state, streaming), API gateway.

\subsection{Filesystem as Write-Ahead Log}
All mutations append to \texttt{.llmwiki/wal/} as JSONL. The directory \texttt{.llmwiki/} is the \emph{sole} durability mechanism: \texttt{rsync} or \texttt{git push} fully captures state. Replay re-ingests the log into empty stores. No Kafka, Redis, or database required.

\subsection{Content Addressing}
Every unit $u$ has $\text{id}(u) = \text{SHA256}(\text{canonical}(u))$. Identical content $\implies$ identical ID across graph, vector index, keyword index, and WAL. Enables deduplication, caching, and verifiable lineage.

\subsection{Evidence Gate}
The evidence gate $\mathcal{G}$ is a mandatory filter: $\mathcal{G}(q, \mathcal{R}, \tau) = \{ r \in \mathcal{R} \mid \text{CE}(q, r) \geq \tau \}$, where $\text{CE}$ is a cross-encoder (e.g., ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2) and $\tau$ is calibrated via isotonic regression on a validation set to control false discovery rate. Unlike rerankers, $\mathcal{G}$ may return $\varnothing$, triggering "insufficient evidence" rather than hallucinated synthesis.

\subsection{DAG Query Planner}
Queries compile to plans $P = (V, E)$ where $v \in V$ is a step (search, traverse, synthesize, tool) and $e \in E$ denotes data dependency. The executor runs topological sort with parallel fan-out. Cost model uses index statistics (vector density, graph degree, BM25 document frequency) to choose retrieval strategies per subquery.

\subsection{Evidence Context Boundary}
After gating, the verified set is selected under the remaining reader budget,
then serialized. The production baseline takes a deterministic score-ordered
prefix. An experimental cost-scaled greedy policy maximises weighted concept
coverage with a best-singleton guard and \texttt{content\_id} tie-breaking; it
cannot replace the baseline before the model A/B in Vol.~06.

The \texttt{WC/1} envelope sends response-local integer handles, source paths
once per group, block kind, line count, and verbatim evidence text. A host-only
map resolves each handle to
\texttt{(content\_id, occurrence\_id, document, span)}. Line-count framing
makes arbitrary evidence unambiguous without escaping or rewriting it. On the
reference fixtures, the complete cold prompt is 30.3\% smaller than JSON for
English and 32.8\% for Korean; after the fixed 52-token contract prefix is
cached, the reductions are 31.7\% and 36.6\%. These measurements use
\texttt{tiktoken o200k\_base} and establish transport cost only.

\subsection{Label-Free Significance via Discordant Pairing}
\label{sec:paper:far}
Let $f$ be the verification scorer. We draw pairs $(q, u^-)$ in which relevance is excluded by construction --- $u^-$ from a domain-disjoint corpus, from a log snapshot predating every entity in $q$, or from a graph component sharing no entity with $q$ --- and accumulate $S_0 = \{f(q,u^-)\}$. The empirical survival function $\hat{F}_0(s) = |\{s' \in S_0 : s' \ge s\}|/|S_0|$ converts any score into a false alarm rate $\text{FAR}(s) = \hat{F}_0(s)\,\bar{k}$, where $\bar{k}$ is the mean candidate count per query, and the operating threshold is the least $s$ attaining a target FAR.

Two properties matter. The procedure consumes no relevance judgements, so recalibration can be triggered automatically as the corpus evolves rather than scheduled around annotation campaigns. And the reported quantity is operational: ``one spurious admission per $N$ queries'' constrains a deployment in a way that a score of $0.72$ does not. The construction bounds spurious admission only; it is silent on recall, and we therefore retain split conformal prediction for coverage where labels exist. The two are complementary, not redundant.

\subsection{Proofreading Cascade}
\label{sec:paper:cascade}
Verification is a sequence of $n$ stages $g_1,\dots,g_n$ with increasing cost, each irreversibly discarding candidates below its threshold. Under conditional independence of stage verdicts given relevance, false acceptance is $\prod_j \varepsilon_j$ and recall $\prod_j \rho_j$, so the discrimination ratio grows as $(\rho/\varepsilon)^n$ for homogeneous stages. Independence is not assumed but engineered: stages read distinct channels --- lexical overlap, cross-encoder relevance, graph reachability among co-survivors, and entailment --- following the parallel-thread principle of \citet{kirby2025parallel}. Expected cost is $c_1 + \sum_{j>1} c_j \prod_{i<j} s_i$ with $s_i$ the stage survival fraction, so terminal stages evaluate a vanishing candidate fraction. As in the thermodynamic setting, the fidelity gain is purchased strictly with dissipation; we report the resulting compute--accuracy frontier rather than an unbounded accuracy claim.

\subsection{Cohomological Evidence Closure (Research Hypothesis)}
\label{sec:paper:sheaf}
Over the surviving set we build a graph joining units that share entities or graph relations, and a cellular sheaf of vector spaces (stalks are claim-confidence vectors over $\mathbb{R}$; restriction maps are entailment/contradiction/neutral-induced linear maps --- see Vol.~04 \S\ref{sec:retrieval:sheaf} for the construction) assigning to each vertex the admissible truth assignments of its atomic claims, to each edge those of the shared claims, and to each incidence a restriction map induced by entailment. The evidence set is closed iff the \emph{specific} edge data induced by the retrieved units' pairwise NLI scores glues to a global section, and obstructed iff that data's class in $\mathrm{coker}\,d$ is nonzero --- this is a statement about one observed assignment, not the unconditional claim ``$H^1(\mathcal{F}) \neq 0$,'' which alone would characterise the sheaf's shape rather than any particular evidence set (Vol.~04 \S\ref{sec:retrieval:sheaf} corrects an earlier draft that conflated the two). On this construction, cyclic inconsistency --- a cycle in which every pair is compatible but no global assignment exists --- produces no pairwise witness, yet is exactly the configuration in which a confidence-weighted system is most confidently wrong; whether the cohomological test catches strictly more such cases than pairwise checking \emph{in practice}, at what NLI-model reliability, and at what compute cost, is an open empirical question this document does not yet answer (Vol.~04 \S\ref{sec:retrieval:sheaf-gaps} lists the unresolved modeling choices: the linearization of truth assignments, uncertainty propagation, and nonlinear logical constraints that vector-space cohomology cannot represent directly). Because $H^1$ is localisable, abstention is accompanied by the specific relations carrying the obstruction. For linear sheaves the computation reduces to kernel and cokernel of the sheaf Laplacian, polynomial in a set already made small by the cascade.

\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:paper:eval}

\subsection{Benchmarks}
We construct \texttt{LLMWikiBench}: six domains, 1000 queries with human-annotated evidence spans:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{CodeQA-Python}: 50 repos, 200 queries (cross-file function calls, inheritance)
  \item \textbf{API-Doc}: 100 OpenAPI specs, 200 queries (parameter constraints, auth flows)
  \item \textbf{SQL-Schema}: 200 schemas, 150 queries (FK paths, constraints, indexes)
  \item \textbf{Tech-Docs}: 500 Markdown/PDF docs, 250 queries (long-context, structure)
  \item \textbf{Config-Files}: 1000 YAML/JSON/TOML, 100 queries (env refs, defaults)
  \item \textbf{Mixed-Corpus}: All above, 100 queries (cross-domain retrieval)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Baselines}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{RAG}: Hybrid vector (BGE-M3) + BM25, top-20, rerank with cross-encoder, generate with Llama-3.1-8B
  \item \textbf{GraphRAG}: Community detection + hierarchical summarization (Edge et al., 2024)
  \item \textbf{HippoRAG}: Personalized PageRank over LLM-extracted graph (Guti{\'e}rrez et al., 2024)
  \item \textbf{LightRAG}: Dual-level retrieval (Guo et al., 2024)
  \item \textbf{RAPTOR}: Recursive tree summarization (Sarthi et al., 2024)
\end{itemize}
All baselines use the same local LLM (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct) for fairness.

\subsection{Metrics}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Evidence Recall@k / Precision@k / F1@k}: Over human-annotated evidence units after gate
  \item \textbf{Attribution Score}: Fraction of answer claims entailed by cited evidence (NLI model)
  \item \textbf{Hallucination Rate}: Claims with no supporting evidence or contradicted by evidence
  \item \textbf{Latency}: End-to-end p50/p95
  \item \textbf{Determinism}: Bitwise answer equality across 10 repeated runs
  \item \textbf{FAR validity}: agreement between the false alarm rate predicted from the empirical null (\S\ref{sec:paper:far}) and the spurious-admission rate measured against held-out human judgements. This is the falsification test for the label-free calibration: if predicted and observed rates diverge, the null is contaminated and the mechanism fails.
  \item \textbf{Cascade frontier}: false-acceptance rate as a function of cascade depth $n$ and of total verification compute, testing whether the multiplicative suppression of \S\ref{sec:paper:cascade} survives the loss of exact stage independence.
  \item \textbf{Cyclic inconsistency detection}: recall of injected three-cycle contradictions --- pairwise-compatible, jointly unsatisfiable --- which pairwise checkers cannot detect by construction, versus the $H^1$ criterion.
  \item \textbf{Abstention quality}: precision of refusals, i.e.\ the fraction of abstentions that a human annotator confirms were unanswerable from the corpus. A gate that abstains often is only valuable if it abstains correctly.
  \item \textbf{Tokens per answered query / refusal token cost}: median complete-prompt tokens for answers and mean complete-prompt tokens for refusals.
  \item \textbf{Coverage per token / budget utilisation}: the selector's weighted concept-coverage proxy divided by tokens, and consumed tokens divided by the configured budget.
  \item \textbf{Quality at budget}: Evidence F1 and Attribution at 25/50/75/100\% budget for the full \texttt{(JSON, WC/1)} $\times$ \texttt{(top-k, packed)} matrix.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Status of empirical claims.} The architecture, algorithms, and guarantees in this document are specified and analytically justified; the benchmark numbers quoted in the abstract and in \S\ref{sec:paper:results} are targets from preliminary runs and are not yet supported by a public, reproducible evaluation. The local reference suite currently passes 91 tests, including deterministic WC/1 round trips, parser-boundary rejection, token-budget enforcement, and context-efficiency metric checks. Those tests establish implementation invariants, not answer quality. In particular, the WC/1-vs-JSON and packed-vs-top-$k$ model A/B has not been run, and the exploratory coverage improvement may not transfer to Evidence F1. We state this explicitly because the mechanisms above are falsifiable and should be judged on measurement rather than on architectural argument. The FAR validity and cyclic-detection metrics are likewise designed to fail loudly if the imported methods do not transfer, and results are reported only once \texttt{LLMWikiBench} and the harness are released.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:paper:discussion}

\subsection{Why Evidence Gate Works}
The gate operationalizes the distinction between \emph{semantic similarity} (vector space proximity) and \emph{evidential relevance} (does this unit actually answer the query?). Cross-encoders attend over the joint (query, unit) pair, capturing fine-grained interactions that bi-encoders miss. Calibration ensures the threshold has probabilistic meaning.

\subsection{Local-First Implications}
By eliminating external API dependencies in the critical path, LLMWiki enables: (1) air-gapped deployment; (2) predictable latency (no queueing, no rate limits); (3) data sovereignty; (4) deterministic replay for audit and debugging.

\subsection{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Cross-encoder inference adds latency (~15ms per batch of 32); mitigated by batching and candidate pruning.
  \item Entity resolution quality depends on embedding quality; poor embeddings $\to$ over/under-merging.
  \item DAG planner requires query decomposition capability; simple queries benefit less.
  \item Evaluation benchmarks are code/document-heavy; less tested on pure natural language corpora.
  \item WC/1 comprehension parity with JSON and the answer-quality effect of concept-coverage packing are unmeasured. Both remain feature-gated until the four-cell quality-at-budget experiment is complete.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:paper:conclusion}

LLMWiki reimagines the RAG pipeline by making evidence verification a mandatory architectural layer rather than an optional metric. The combination of content-addressed units, filesystem-backed WAL, graph-first knowledge representation, cross-encoder evidence gate, and DAG planning is designed to be more accurate, more trustworthy, and more auditable than prior art while running entirely on local hardware; \S\ref{sec:paper:results} states this as a falsifiable target pending release of \texttt{LLMWikiBench} and a reproducible evaluation, not as an established result.
